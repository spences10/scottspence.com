# Let's work together

<Small>

I help teams build and operate AI products, coding-agent
infrastructure, and SvelteKit applications.

That can mean shipping a new workflow, building MCP tools and evals,
or adding scope, validation, observability, and human-review controls
to agent-assisted engineering. I work through
[OES Technology](https://oestechnology.co.uk) from Kent, UK, with
clients in the UK and internationally.

## Problems I can help with

### Production AI products

I build product workflows that combine user interfaces, document and
data processing, model calls, human review, audit trails, and the
platform controls needed to operate them reliably.

This is product engineering, not a prompt demo. I can work across the
SvelteKit interface, service boundaries, agent execution, evals,
observability, deployment, and release checks.

### Coding-agent infrastructure

I help engineering teams use coding agents without lowering their
standards. My work covers enforceable task scope, deterministic
validation, LSP tooling, session recall, MCP integration, secret-safe
execution, evals, telemetry, and human review.

I use these systems in real repositories and publish the evidence,
including [coding-agent harnesses with my-pi], [preventing agent
drift], and [building a SQLite context sidecar].

### Svelte and SvelteKit engineering

I've worked with Svelte and SvelteKit for <DD date="2021-04-06" /> and
am a recognised Svelte Ambassador. I can help with product
architecture, Svelte 5 migrations, large codebases, testing,
accessibility, performance, shared UI systems, and developer
experience.

For work focused only on Svelte, see
[svelteconsulting.dev](https://svelteconsulting.dev).

### Technical leadership

I previously led 12 developers across two product teams building AI
customer-service software in a large SvelteKit monorepo. I can lead a
project, work alongside an existing team, establish delivery controls,
or take ownership of a difficult technical area.

## How I work

I prefer long-term relationships where I can understand the product,
ship useful work, and stay involved as it reaches production. Focused
audits, prototypes, workshops, and short delivery projects can also be
a good starting point.

You can expect:

- direct communication and clear trade-offs;
- small, reviewable changes rather than mystery rewrites;
- accessibility, performance, tests, and release checks from the
  start;
- evidence for technical claims and honest limits;
- documentation that lets your team own the work afterwards.
